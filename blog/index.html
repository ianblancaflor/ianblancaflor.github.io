2026/11/06

last night i had 5 hours of sleep, cause yesterday was a lot. i went to walmart to buy ocarina of time, times, costco, safeway... then we cooked a vodka pasta with beef, and i played OoT from 9-10:30. then i went on a 5 mile run and finished up at 11:30, and as a result was restless. i played OoT until i fell asleep at about 1:40, then left for work extra early cause i thought i had to (i didn't). basically what we're gonna do is watch the strokes guitar tutorials, logic session breakdowns from charli xcx, and use my mental energy to ruminate on potential projects as opposed to... doing it. so here's me starting the doing it by listing out everything I need to do


first off i need to work on my birthday party. this includes the theming, coordinating my friends to DJ, curating my set, creating the activities, etc. a few years ago i threw a DS themed party, where people were split into teams and we had them do crazy challenges like warioware poses and multiplication tables (it was awesome). anyways, despite being a private event, it's fun to go all out on it. feels like a practice run for public events like the untitled music festival i organized for late august. i did DDR 2 years ago which was pretty awesome, then i held my first performance/public DJ set a year ago which was incredibly awesome. it was definitely amatuer (i still am) but it truly was a launching point for me getting into this local scene and meeting so many of the people that i know today. a surprise to me is that most of the people i met aren't a majority musicians, but a broad range of creative which i find fascinating even though i'm not a part of it (fashion, video making, etc.). just these few years have been the most life changingly significant, and oftentimes when referring to the past i use these birthday parties as timestamps. going all out for these parties feels like a duty i have, to preserve a moment that in hindsight will become definitive.


second off, i need to work on new music. it FEELS like i'm so busy all the time doing things, but truly mapping out&analyzing my time spent it definitely hasn't been productive. and i despise the idea that every hour of the day must be working towards SOMETHING (after all i loved playing ocarina of time last night and will continue to do so). instead it is doing everything intentionally. whenever i lack sleep i tend to fall back to old habits, things that are easier to do that are less physically and mentally demanding (DOOMSCROLL). sleep deprivation = less quality work/continual grogginess = wait until i'm rested. the solution to this problem is pretty simple: prioritize sleep. i definitely need 7-8 hours of sleep and exercise in the morning, as i continuously relearn that these two things make the rest of my day feel awesome. with marathon training fully kicking in for the race on december 13th, i'll have to really dial in my schedule so i can spend quality time with my partner, my friends, and the music.


third, i need to work on soundOclock (AND MAYBE GET A VOCAL COACH??? hmmm....) it's gonna be my first live show since opening for steven, and i'm ready to put my all into perfecting THE SHOW after my birthday party and once we're staying in town for 2 weeks. now what does THE SHOW include, and why do i feel so meh about my steven performance? the first priority is REHEARSING THE MATERIAL, and playing it straight through a couple times doesn't count. i have to play through every song a couple times, see what i want to perform and what i want to leave as a backing track, then truthfully learn and memorize the parts. this will probably take hours and hours of playing a song straight through, taking note of the parts i didn't play/sing to satisfaction, etc. maybe i'll finally print a set list to tape it on the floor


i feel that writing this out has given me a nice break from IT ALL. gonna use this momentum to update other parts of this website now, bye bye.
